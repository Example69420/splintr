# 🔊 soundkey-flipperzero - Sound Made Simple for Everyone

Welcome to **soundkey-flipperzero**! This application transforms your Flipper Zero device into an accessible audio companion. If you are blind, have low vision, or simply prefer sound-based controls, SoundKey is here to help you navigate and use your device with confidence. No technical skills needed—just download, run, and listen.

---

## ✅ What Is SoundKey?

SoundKey is a free, open-source audio interface that adds spoken feedback, sound cues, and simple controls to your Flipper Zero. Instead of relying on tiny screens or complicated menus, SoundKey speaks to you, plays helpful tones, and makes every action clear through sound. Whether you are checking the battery, exploring files, or using tools, SoundKey turns your Flipper Zero into a friendlier, more inclusive device.

---

## 🎯 Who Is This For?

- **Blind and low-vision users** who want full access to their Flipper Zero.
- **Beginners** who find traditional menus confusing.

## 🧭 How to Navigate with SoundKey

SoundKey replaces visual menus with spoken ones. Here is a quick guide:

- **Arrow Up/Down** – Move between options.
- **Arrow Left/Right** – Change settings or values.
- **Enter** – Select or confirm.
- **Escape** – Go back one step.
- **Spacebar** – Pause or resume speech.
- **F2** – Repeat the last spoken message.

Every action produces a sound cue. A **rising tone** means success, a **low buzz** means error, and a **ding** means you have reached the end of a list. These sounds are short and pleasant, so you always know where you are.
